Why I’m Running

It’s Time to Refocus on What Matters.

As a current Special Education Department Chair, Pre-K Team Lead, and Princeton parent, I have seen firsthand how school board decisions directly impact classrooms, teachers, students, and families.

I believe Princeton ISD deserves leadership that is grounded in today’s classrooms. My campaign is focused on measurable student success and practical classroom improvements—not political talking points.

Every board decision should directly improve opportunities for our students and support our hard-working teachers. That is what effective board leadership is about.

"As an educator, I don’t have the luxury of debating problems all day. Every morning, my students walk through the classroom door expecting me to help them succeed. I believe our school board should approach every decision with that same sense of purpose."

What Does "Results Over Rhetoric" Mean?

Results Over Rhetoric means focusing on what works to improve classrooms—not simply what creates headlines or talking points.

Rhetoric is:

  • Talking without improving.
  • Debating without solving.
  • Explaining instead of fixing.
  • Measuring success by headlines instead of student growth.

Results are:

  • More students reading on grade level.
  • Stronger math achievement.
  • Better Special Education services.
  • Better teacher support.
  • Honest communication with families.
  • Transparent decision-making.
  • Graduates prepared for college, careers, and life.

My Promise to Princeton

Values Should Be Visible in Our Results.

Values matter. But on a school board, values should become action. If we value students, we improve learning. If we value teachers, we support them. If we value parents, we communicate honestly. If we value taxpayers, we are transparent and accountable.

As a trustee on the school board, I promise to evaluate every proposed policy, budget item, and decision by asking five simple questions before casting my vote. If the answer is yes, I’ll support it.

Before every vote, I will ask:

  • ✓ Will this help students learn?
  • ✓ Will this support teachers?
  • ✓ Will this strengthen our schools?
  • ✓ Will this improve Special Education?
  • ✓ Will this prepare students for tomorrow?

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about Charlotte's background and campaign goals.

Results Over Rhetoric means focusing on what works in classrooms. School boards exist to improve schools—not simply debate them or chase headlines. For Charlotte, every decision should directly lead to academic growth, teacher retention, stronger special education resources, and transparent budgeting rather than talking points.

Charlotte is not only a local parent—she is a current educator, Pre-K Team Lead, and Special Education Department Chair with more than a decade of experience in Texas public schools. She has worked inside Princeton ISD and currently leads SPED teams. She understands how district board policies affect teachers, students, and classrooms because she lives those realities every single day.

Charlotte's four main priorities are: 1. Student Academic Achievement First (especially reading and math), 2. Special Education Support and Screenings, 3. Supporting Teachers to reduce classroom burnout, and 4. Campus-by-Campus transparency and accountability for district goals and tax expenditures.

Charlotte believes every student should be treated with dignity, every parent deserves to be heard, and every school must follow the law. As a trustee, she will keep the board's primary focus on the issues that most directly improve student learning, support teachers, and manage district growth responsibly—minimizing outside political distractions.
