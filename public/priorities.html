My Priorities

RESULTS OVER RHETORIC

Princeton ISD is growing and changing. Our leadership must focus on what works in classrooms to improve opportunities for every child.

1

Student Achievement First

Students deserve a school board that measures success by academic growth and student outcomes—not simply district summaries or political arguments.

Charlotte is committed to ensuring every campus in Princeton ISD has the tools and trackers necessary to help students achieve key benchmarks, from early phonics to secondary career readiness.

Charlotte will focus on:

  • ✓ Reading & Literacy: Implementing evidence-based trackers for early phonics.
  • ✓ Mathematics & Science: Building solid quantitative skills.
  • ✓ Early Intervention: Finding and correcting educational gaps before they widen.
  • ✓ Future-Ready Classrooms: Responsible tech integration and AI-workforce preparation.
  • ✓ Data-Driven Checks: Making district policies match proven classroom results.

"Our students don’t need better explanations. They need better outcomes."

— Charlotte Wilson

2

Leadership Informed by Experience

School board decisions affect classrooms every day. As a current classroom educator, Special Education Department Chair, and Pre-K Team Lead, Charlotte lives these challenges every day.

Leadership should be informed by classroom reality—not assumptions. She understands how board-level compliance policies affect teacher burnout, classroom resources, and special education classrooms.

Classroom experience means:

  • ✓ Knowing exactly what classroom teachers need to succeed.
  • ✓ Navigating state special education compliance rules inside schools.
  • ✓ Having served and worked directly inside Princeton ISD.
  • ✓ Understanding how policy changes actually impact families.

"School board decisions belong in classrooms—not just boardrooms."

— Charlotte Wilson

3

Transparency Builds Trust

Families deserve honest communication. Teachers deserve transparency. Taxpayers deserve accountability.

Charlotte believes parents should have access to meaningful information about their local schools—not just district-wide summaries that smooth over problem areas. She supports campus-by-campus metrics and transparent planning.

Charlotte supports:

  • ✓ Campus-by-Campus Transparency: Tracking local reading and math success.
  • ✓ Clear Student Achievement Data: Easily understood dashboards for parents.
  • ✓ Measurable District Goals: Aligning budgets directly with academic growth.
  • ✓ Public Accountability: Transparent board communications and voting records.

"Transparency builds trust. Trust builds stronger schools."

— Charlotte Wilson

4

Strong Special Education

Every child deserves the opportunity to succeed. As a Special Education teacher and Department Chair, Charlotte understands that students need more than legal compliance on paper.

They need meaningful services, early screening, and supported teachers who have the resources to implement IEPs effectively. Special Education is not a separate issue—it is public education.

Charlotte's SPED commitment:

  • ✓ Meaningful Services: Moving beyond simple checklist compliance.
  • ✓ Early Identification: Strengthening screenings at the Pre-K level.
  • ✓ Educator Resources: Providing direct training and SPED assistants.
  • ✓ Family Collaboration: Ensuring open communication between parents and school teams.

"Every student deserves more than a plan on paper. They deserve every opportunity to succeed."

— Charlotte Wilson

Charlotte Wilson

Grounded in experience, focused on results. Refocusing Princeton ISD on student success and classroom outcomes.
