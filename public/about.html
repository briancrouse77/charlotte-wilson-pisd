Meet Charlotte Wilson

Dedicated Educator. Princeton Parent. Advocate for Students.

Charlotte Wilson is a Special Education teacher, current Special Education Department Chair, current Pre-K Team Lead, former Princeton ISD teacher, and Princeton parent with more than a decade of experience serving students in Texas public schools.

She currently serves in Garland ISD, where she leads her Special Education team and her Pre-K teaching team while supporting students with diverse learning needs. Previously, Charlotte taught in Princeton ISD, giving her firsthand knowledge of the district she is now seeking to serve. Throughout her career, she has worked alongside teachers, administrators, specialists, and families to solve problems, improve instruction, and advocate for students.

Charlotte believes school board leadership should be informed by today’s classrooms—not yesterday’s assumptions. She is running to help ensure Princeton ISD remains focused on student achievement, teacher support, transparency, and preparing every child for the future.

"School board decisions belong in classrooms—not just boardrooms."

My Values

Values Should Be Visible in Our Results.

Values matter. But on a school board, values must become action. Charlotte's commitments are defined by measurable student learning and taxpayer transparency.

If we value students —

We improve learning outcomes, support reading proficiency, and ensure early academic intervention.

If we value teachers —

We support them with resources, reduce unnecessary administrative layers, and respect their classroom feedback.

If we value parents —

We communicate honestly, make information understandable, and build campus-specific transparency.

If we value taxpayers —

We maintain full fiscal accountability, align budgets with student needs, and transparently explain board expenditures.

"My values are measured by whether students are learning."

Charlotte Wilson

Grounded in experience, focused on results. Refocusing Princeton ISD on student success and classroom outcomes.
